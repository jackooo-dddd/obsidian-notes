nuScenes is one of the most famous datasets in autonomous driving. It contains 1,000 real driving scenes, each about 20 seconds, collected in Boston and Singapore. Data contains 3D bounding-box annotations and maps.
The input data contains camera / LiDAR / Radar, dataset can be used to train the Model for object detection, object tracking or trajectory prediction.

nuScenes is **open-loop**, the model does not actually drive the car in nuScenes. Model just predict what the car would do from a recorded scene.
